# 🎨 HoverHue VR

**HoverHue VR** is an experimental VR painting game that combines **locomotion, creativity, and challenge** into one colorful experience.  
Ride your hoverboard, paint the world, and race against a rainbow sphere before it colors everything for you!

---

## 🕹️ Gameplay Overview

In *HoverHue VR*, you move and interact through innovative VR mechanics:

- 🛹 **Hoverboard Locomotion** — Move left or right by **tilting your VR controllers** while gliding through the world.  
- 🎨 **Dynamic Painting System** — Use your **paintbrush** to color blocks and **sample colors directly from the environment**.  
- 🌈 **Race Against Time** — A **rainbow sphere** is spreading color across the map — once it touches a block, you lose your chance to paint it!  
- ⚙️ **Adaptive Difficulty** — Change the challenge level using your paintbrush:  
  - Paint **red blocks** to **increase difficulty**  
  - Paint **blue blocks** to **lower difficulty**  
  - Choose your level right at the **start screen**  
- 🗣️ **Voice-Guided Instructions** — Learn the mechanics through **in-game narration** at the beginning of each session.

---

## 🧠 Core Features

| Feature | Description |
|----------|--------------|
| 🎯 Intuitive hoverboard movement | Turning or tilting your VR controllers smoothly steers your movement. |
| 🖌️ Interactive painting brush | Sample colors from the world and apply them to blocks with realistic blending. |
| 🌈 Reactive world | The rainbow sphere dynamically paints blocks, forcing quick and strategic decisions. |
| 🔊 Voice instructions | Fully narrated tutorial that guides players through gameplay and settings. |
| ⚖️ Paint-based difficulty system | Change game difficulty through the color you choose to paint at the start screen. |

---

## 🧩 Tech & Tools

- **Engine:** Unity (XR Interaction Toolkit)
- **Language:** C#
- **Platform:** Meta Quest 3 (Standalone, OpenXR)
- **Audio:** Unity’s Audio Mixer for dynamic narration
- **Physics:** Unity’s Rigidbody & Collider systems for hoverboard and projectile simulation

---

## 🧱 Controls

| Action | Control |
|---------|----------|
| Move left/right | Tilt or turn VR controllers |
| Paint | Use the trigger while brush touches a block |
| Sample color | Touch a colored surface with your brush tip |
| Adjust difficulty | Paint red (harder) or blue (easier) on the start screen |
| Start the game | Follow the voice instructions after setting difficulty |

---

## 🧭 Objective

> **Paint every block before the rainbow sphere does!**

Plan your moves, master your hoverboard balance, and react quickly to outpaint the spreading colors.

## 📦 Installation (Quest 3)

1. Download the latest `.apk` release from the [Itch.io page](https://n9ano.itch.io/) or this repository’s **Releases** section.  
2. Open **SideQuest Desktop** on your PC.  
3. Connect your **Meta Quest 3** via USB-C and enable **Developer Mode**.  
4. Click **“Install APK”** and select `HoverHueVR_v1.0.apk`.  
5. Launch the game from **Unknown Sources** on your headset.
